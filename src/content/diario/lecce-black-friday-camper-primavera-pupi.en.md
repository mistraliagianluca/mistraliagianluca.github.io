Black Friday is Friday 27 November, and we are starting early: from Tuesday 24 November to Tuesday 1 December. The offer is simple. A discount on the Kentucky, to set off in spring.

## The discount at a glance

It's 15% off Kentucky rentals starting between 1 March and 31 May 2027. You book on our website from 24 November to 1 December. Prices start from €78 a day.

We have one motorhome, so the best dates fill up fast. That window includes Easter (28 March), 25 April, 1 May and 2 June. They are great candidates for a long weekend of 3-4 days. But the discount isn't tied to a holiday: you choose when to go.

## And the destination? Wherever you want

The offer isn't tied to any destination. The Kentucky leaves from Modugno and goes wherever you like: sea, villages, parks, campsites with a view. We take you wherever you want, really.

One idea among many: Lecce, about 2 hours' drive (depending on traffic) from Bari. Baroque in the morning, Salento coast in the afternoon, dinner on board.

If you don't want to wait until March, from 5 to 27 December 2026 the Chiostro dei Teatini hosts the Fiera dei Pupi, also known as the Santa Lucia Fair, promoted by the Municipality of Lecce. It is an exhibition and market of nativity scenes and figures in papier-mâché and terracotta made by local artisans. Check the up-to-date opening hours before you go. The discount does not apply to December rentals.

To park in Lecce, campers report using the ex Foro Boario car park in Piazza Carmelo Bene. It is paid and unattended, about 1-1.5 km from the centre, with a shuttle. Check rates on site and don't leave valuables on board.

## What's in the price

Everything you need is in the price. You set off with a full tank, with 100 km per night included, a kitchen kit, table and chairs and 24/7 roadside assistance. Bed linen is an extra.

The Kentucky is a Ford Transit sleeping 4+1, with two double beds always ready and a fifth seat in the dinette. It has a full kitchen, a bathroom with separate shower, air conditioning, diesel heating and an inverter. It also works well for free camping. Before every departure we check the mechanics, fluid levels and hygiene.
