## Europe's oldest Carnival starts on Boxing Day

On Saturday 26 December 2026 Putignano opens the 633rd edition of its Carnival, titled «Smascherata! Liberate le maschere» (Unmasked! Free the masks). The Festa delle Propaggini kicks it off. Poets recite satirical verses in local dialect, in traditional costume, and the town makes fun of itself. According to the Foundation's calls for entries, the verses are judged by a jury of citizens picked by lot and by the public through the official app.

Times and venues for 26 December have not been published yet. The full programme is due in the coming weeks, so check the official Fondazione Carnevale di Putignano website before you leave.

## A long weekend from Bari

Putignano is about 45 minutes to 1 hour from Bari. That makes it an easy long weekend of 3-4 days between Christmas and Boxing Day. Our minimum rental is 2-3 days, so the format fits.

What to put on your list:

- **The Propaggini**: arrive early and enjoy the dialect verses. Even if you miss some words, you'll get the jokes.
- **Papier-mâché**: it is the heart of Putignano's Carnival. The floats parading in February stand over 12 metres tall and are made of papier-mâché.
- **The Itria Valley**: Alberobello, Locorotondo and Martina Franca are all close. In December the lights and the crisp air make everything prettier.

Late December evenings are chilly. The Kentucky has diesel heating, two double beds always ready and a full kitchen. It is also equipped for free camping, with an inverter and a separate shower. Where you stop is up to you.

## When to come back

26 December is only the beginning. The float parades are on 31 January, 7 February, 9 February (Shrove Tuesday) and 13 February. The Carnival season lasts seven weeks. If the Propaggini sound fun, come back in February for the floats.
