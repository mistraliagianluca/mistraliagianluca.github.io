On Saturday 12 December 2026, at 6 pm, Agnone lights the **Grand 'Ndocciata**. The 'ndocce are fir-wood torches that can be over three metres tall. They are carried down the main street by men in the black capes of the local farming tradition, and end up in a final bonfire known as the bonfire of brotherhood. It's one of the most spectacular fire rituals in Europe and is recognised as part of Italy's traditional heritage. And yes, it's cold. That's why our camper has heating.

## Getting there and how long to stay

Agnone is **about 3.5-4 hours from Bari**. Take the A14 north to Vasto Sud, then the Trignina road up the Trigno valley into Alto Molise. This is mountain country, so check the forecast. In winter, many roads require winter tyres or snow chains on board.

Our advice: **4-5 days**, for example from Thursday 10 to Monday 14 December. Drive up at an easy pace, enjoy the 'Ndocciata evening, then spend the remaining days exploring. If you can't make it on the 12th, Agnone holds the ritual again on Christmas Eve, on Thursday 24 December at 6 pm, on a smaller scale.

## Where to park

Agnone is friendly to campervans. It has **two free municipal camper areas, open all year**. One is near the hospital and the stadium (SP86) and is slightly sloped. The other is near the bus terminal and is flat. The area near the hospital has free water and waste disposal; both have electric hook-ups, but electricity is charged. The centre is walking distance. On festival days, arrive early because spaces are limited. The Kentucky has its own bathroom with a separate shower anyway. With an inverter and diesel heating, a night off-grid is comfortable too.

## What to see

- **The Pontifical Marinelli Foundry** and its museum, where bells have been cast for centuries. Check opening hours before you go.
- **Agnone's old town**, with stone churches and doorways. Try the caciocavallo cheese and local sweets.
- **Pietrabbondante**, a short drive away, with its Samnite theatre set into the mountainside.
- **The silver fir woods** towards Pescopennataro, for a walk in cold, clean air.
