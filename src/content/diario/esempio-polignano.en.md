Polignano a Mare is half an hour from Bari, the ideal first stop to get to know the Kentucky. Off-season the town is quiet, the sea shows its best colours and parking is easy.

## What to see
- **Lama Monachile**, the tiny cove between the rocks that symbolises the town
- **The old town**, with terraces hanging over the sea
- **The sea caves**, best seen by boat on a calm day

## Where to stay
There are equipped campervan areas just outside the centre: arrive in the afternoon, have dinner in town and wake up early for sunrise on the cliffs.

The Kentucky has everything on board: kitchen, shower and heating. You just bring the sheets.
